Why each part is there (the gate reads the caption only):

- `Made by my AI agent for a $500 bounty on make.fun, ...` — the required
  line, verbatim. It carries `$500 bounty` and make.fun, both required.
- `makefun-planter-amber-kiwi` — our make.fun play code for this bounty
  (code 0 of 4). The caption or the account bio must carry one of our play
  codes; the caption is the surest place.
- `https://make.fun/r/rftzw4en` — our referral link. Not required by the gate
  and not read by it, but it is the point of the ad: viewers who join
  through it are our referrals.
- Hashtags are for TikTok discovery only.

## Posting steps (the user does this — the agent has no TikTok account)

1. Open TikTok on the phone (app, or tiktok.com in a browser) on a PUBLIC
   account — Settings and privacy → Privacy → Private account OFF.
2. Tap + / Upload, pick `tiktok-ad.mp4`.
3. Do NOT edit the video in TikTok: no trim, no filter, no added sound, no
   text sticker, no effect. The bounty disqualifies a video edited by hand
   in TikTok's editor. Post the file exactly as rendered.
4. Paste the caption above exactly. Double-check the play code
   `makefun-planter-amber-kiwi` is spelled right.
5. Set "Who can watch this video" to **Everyone**.
6. Post. Then open the video in a browser while signed out and copy its
   full address: `https://www.tiktok.com/@<handle>/video/<id>`.
   A short share link (vm.tiktok.com / vt.tiktok.com / tiktok.com/t/) does
   NOT count — open it in a browser and copy the full address it lands on.
7. Hand that address to the agent: it goes into VIDEO.json, then the entry
   is submitted. The video must stay public and viewable in the US until
   the results are out (due 9 Dec 2026).

## What NOT to do

- Never buy views, never use TikTok Promote or any paid promotion, never
  loop the video on your own devices or accounts. Only views from real
  people count; bought, botted or farmed views disqualify the entry.
- Sharing the video and asking real people to watch it is fine — that is
  exactly how it earns views.
- Do not delete, privatise, or restrict the video before the results.
